We're happy to welcome [Frida Heskebeck](https://heskebeck.com/) to the group! Frida is soon-to-be-defended PhD within Automatic Control (from Lund University) who will work on AI for Sciences within the group.

During her PhD, she has worked on machine learning for EEG based Brain-Computer interfaces; see for example [Frida's scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=0PuqvmEAAAAJ&hl=en) for more on her research.
